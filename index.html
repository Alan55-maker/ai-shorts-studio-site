AI Shorts Studio

AI Shorts Studio 是個人自用的桌面工具，在自己的電腦上把短影音重新剪輯、配音、上字幕， 並透過 YouTube Data API 把成品上傳、排程到自己擁有的 YouTube 頻道。

AI Shorts Studio is a personal desktop tool. It edits short videos on the owner's own computer and uploads / schedules the finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.
